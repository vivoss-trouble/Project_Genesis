# Genesis v18.0 Long-Clock Business Mutation Loop Report

## Status

Implemented the v18.0 local long-clock business mutation controller, fixture,
probe, and validation gate.

## Decision

v18.0 uses a symmetrical local fixture instead of a public page. This keeps
network variability out of the experiment and concentrates pressure on the
actual risk: repeated business mutation across identical pages without stale
focus, stale form data, or stale AX node reuse.

The controller keeps v17.0b as the combobox source of truth. During v18.0, the
business assertion is intentionally centered on text entry, checkbox state, and
commit-result remap. That keeps the long-clock test from re-opening the Z-axis
combobox problem already sealed by v17.0b.

## Scope

This is a local deterministic long-clock test, not a public network test. It
does not persist business data and does not cross domains. Its purpose is to
prove that business mutation can be inserted into a repeated ISR-protected
pagination loop while preserving clean remap boundaries and a legal terminal
stop.
